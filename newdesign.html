The School Companion programme

A child's future shouldn't depend on their postcode.

In remote villages, getting to school is only the beginning. Children also need someone who notices when they struggle, encourages them to try, and helps them stay.

That's why we place trained, full-time School Companions inside rural government schools. Not just for a visit. For every school day.

Where does Vazhai work?

Our work spans Krishnagiri, Dharmapuri, and Villupuram in Tamil Nadu, with a current focus on remote forest and hill-area government schools in Krishnagiri. Explore the areas we work in.

What is a School Companion?

A trained, full-time Vazhai employee based in a rural government school. They support learning, track attendance, work with the headmaster, and connect with families to help prevent dropout.

Is Vazhai a registered organisation?

Yes. Vazhai was founded in April 2005 and is registered in Tamil Nadu, India, under Registration Number 296/05. Get to know Vazhai.

Are donations eligible for tax deduction?

Vazhai does not currently hold 80G exemption. Donations are not eligible for Indian income tax deduction. You can find donation information on our donation page.
