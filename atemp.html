VAZHAI believes education is the path to a more equal society — a future where a child's opportunities are not decided by their village, their background, or their family income. We place trained, full-time School Companions inside the schools that are most forgotten.

The Problem
01

Geography as isolation

Schools sit 20–30 km inside forest roads with no public transport. Teachers don't stay long in such schools. Many primary schools have only one teacher covering every grade.

02

Silent dropout

Parents migrate for daily wages and sometimes take their children with them. Dropout doesn't announce itself — a child just stops showing up, and no one follows up.

03

First-generation learners with no roadmap

These children have no one at home who's been through school, college, or a career. They don't know what's possible, so they don't reach for it.

04

Foundational gaps that compound

A child who can't read fluently by Class 5 rarely catches up on their own. Without targeted support, the gap widens every year.

Our Story · Values · People

Who We Are

Founded April 2005 · Reg. No. 296/05

First-generation graduates building for the next generation

VAZHAI was founded in Chennai by first-generation college graduates from Presidency College who knew what it cost to be the first in a family to reach higher education — and chose to lower that cost for everyone who came after. Many of our team members grew up in the same conditions as the students we serve.

"We were first-generation graduates ourselves. We knew the confusion, the lack of guidance, the moments where you almost gave up. We started Vazhai so other children wouldn't have to go through that alone."
Vazhai Founding Team · Presidency College, Chennai · Est. April 2005
The Name

Why வாழை?

🌿
Vazhai means banana tree in Tamil — it gives completely and regenerates on its own.

Every part of the banana tree serves a purpose. It sends out new shoots that carry the same strength as the parent. We chose it deliberately: one child who gets an education passes that possibility on to the next generation — in Krishnagiri, Dharmapuri, Villupuram, and beyond.

Our program doesn't focus on one subject or one exam. We build literacy, confidence, career awareness, and community trust — because a child needs all of it to thrive. And like the banana tree, this is built to last.

Our Three Principles
I
Generational Growth
One child who gets an education passes that possibility on to the next. We are building that cycle, one school at a time.
II
Complete Nourishment
We build literacy, confidence, career awareness, and community trust — because a child needs all of it to thrive.
III
Built to Last
Not a project. A permanent commitment to rural education equity — one framework, many schools, sustained presence.

Where We Work

Krishnagiri · Dharmapuri · Villupuram

Between 2005 and 2024, Vazhai served students in Dharmapuri and Villupuram. In 2025, we expanded into the forest and hill region beyond Aiyur, near Denkanikottai in Krishnagiri district — where some villages sit 20–30 km inside forest routes with no public transport.

We began with Kodakarai Secondary School. By the end of 2026, we aim to have 10 rural government schools covered — not a workshop that comes and goes, but a person who stays.
