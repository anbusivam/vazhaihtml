Three Roles · One Mission

What We Do

Getting trained, committed people into rural government schools in Tamil Nadu — and keeping them there, every day.

🏫
Full-time · In-School Employee

School Companions

₹15–20K/mo
▾

A School Companion is Vazhai's most direct investment in a child. Present every school day in a remote government school, they build trust with families and ensure no student quietly slips away.

✓
Reading, writing & speaking assessed 3× a year; 25% improvement target Required
✓
Every student speaks on stage at least once a year Required
✓
Dropout tracking — identify absent students early, work with families Required
✓
Weekly & annual reports submitted every Friday Required
→
Home visits — build parent trust, understand barriers
→
Library & daily reading sessions
→
Bridge course for students below grade level
→
NMMS scholarship guidance

Full-time · Field Employee

Field Coordinators

₹25–35K/mo
▾

The Field Coordinator travels to each school weekly, supports Companions, and brings in outside resources — career experts, inter-school competitions, CSR partners.

✓
Visit at least one school weekly; review Companion reports Required
✓
Identify and document new rural schools for the program Required
✓
Train teachers and Companions in student level identification Required
✓
Career guidance sessions in every school, every year Required
→
Plan inter-school sports and cultural competitions
→
Coordinate skill exposure visits (e.g. TVS plant tours)
→
Meet donors; support CSR fundraising partnerships

Where We Work

Krishnagiri · Dharmapuri · Villupuram

Between 2005 and 2024, Vazhai served students in Dharmapuri and Villupuram. In 2025, we expanded into the forest and hill region beyond Aiyur, near Denkanikottai in Krishnagiri district — where some villages sit 20–30 km inside forest routes.

We began with Kodakarai Secondary School. By the end of 2026, we aim to have 10 rural government schools covered — not a workshop that comes and goes, but a person who stays.
