VAZHAI வாழை · An Educational NGO (Reg No. 296/05)
Deep inside the forests of the Krishnagiri district, beyond Denkanikottai and further still past Aiyur, lie small villages where government schools quietly do their best. Teachers make long daily commutes through forest routes because settling here isn't easy. Parents travel far for work, and children sometimes go with them. The nearest town for essentials can be hours away.
The children growing up here are no different from children anywhere — curious, playful, full of potential. What they need is someone who shows up every day, stays, and believes in them. That is what a Vazhai School Companion does. We are working to place one in every school that needs it — starting now, growing every year.

The context these children grow up in

🌲 Villages like Kodakarai lie 20–30 km inside forest areas beyond Aiyur — with limited or no public transport. Some areas have one bus a day; some have none at all.
🏫 Teachers who are posted here often commute from towns like Denkanikottai or Hosur because settling in the forest is not practical — meaning the school does not always have a stable, daily adult presence.
👨‍👩‍👧 With limited local livelihoods, many parents travel to nearby towns or nurseries for work — and children sometimes go with them, breaking the rhythm of schooling.
💡 The children here are curious and playful. What they need is a consistent, caring presence — someone who shows up every day, knows their name, and keeps the spark of learning alive.

Who We Are

Our story, values & people
VAZHAI வாழை · An Educational NGO (Reg No. 296/05)

Our Genesis

Vazhai was born out of gratitude and a deep sense of responsibility. Founded in April 2005 by First Generation College Graduates from Presidency College, Chennai — people who had walked the difficult path from rural disadvantage to higher education, and chose to pay that forward.

Many of our team members are themselves from similar backgrounds. That lived experience shapes everything: how we speak to students, what we mentor them on, and why we never give up on a child others might have written off.

We were first generation graduates ourselves. We knew what it felt like to be the first in a family to reach college, the confusion, the lack of guidance, and the moments where you almost felt like giving up. We started Vazhai so other children wouldn’t have to go through that alone.
🌱
Vazhai Founding Team
First Generation Graduates, Presidency College Chennai · Est. April 2005

The Meaning of Vazhai

வாழை (Vazhai) — the Tamil word for the banana tree — carries deep symbolism for us. At its heart, Vazhai stands for bringing equality through education to rural government school students who deserve the same opportunities as anyone else.

Generational Growth: Just as a banana tree produces offshoots that carry the lineage forward, education creates a continuous cycle of growth across generations — one child who gets support eventually helps the next.

Nourishment: Every part of the banana tree serves a purpose. Our holistic approach nourishes every aspect of a child's development — academic, social, and personal.

Sustainability: The banana tree regenerates. Our School Companion model is built to do the same — a system that scales to new schools year after year, not events that happen once and leave.

Our Philosophy

Vazhai's core belief is simple: every child deserves an equal chance at education — regardless of where they were born, how remote their village is, or what their parents do for a living. We exist to bring that equality to rural government schools in Tamil Nadu, one school at a time.

Our team consists of volunteers from diverse professional backgrounds — engineers, doctors, teachers, entrepreneurs, and social workers — united by the conviction that geography should not determine destiny.

Our Journey: Then & Now

From 2005 to 2024, Vazhai ran continuous mentoring programs in Dharmapuri and Villupuram — building deep relationships with students through workshops, home visits, and career guidance. Over 500 students benefited, and many went on to pursue higher education and careers they hadn't imagined before.

As Vazhai matured, we asked: how do we reach more children, more deeply, more sustainably? The answer was a new model — placing School Companions full-time inside government schools in remote hill and forest regions, where the need is greatest and the reach is hardest. A model built not just for impact, but for scale.

School Companions
Employee · Placed directly in rural government schools

School Companions are Vazhai's most direct link to students. Placed full-time inside schools in remote forest and hill regions, they work alongside teachers and the community, and report weekly to the Vazhai network. Our first companion at Kodakarai Secondary School (2025) is from the same community as the students — which has made a real difference in trust and connection.

  • Reading, Writing & Speaking: Improve foundational literacy and communication skills Mandatory — assessed at the start, middle, and end of the year, targeting 25% improvement or a score above 70%
  • Student Stage Speech: Build confidence through classroom presentations Mandatory — each student speaks at least once a year, recorded and shared with the Vazhai network
  • Assist the Headmaster: Support day-to-day school functioning — taking classes, assisting in special sessions, and helping organise school events Mandatory
  • Baseline Assessment: Conduct initial evaluations of each student's academic level to plan targeted support Mandatory
  • Dropout Tracking: Identify students who have stopped attending; engage with families to understand reasons and support re-enrolment Mandatory
  • House Visits: Regularly visit students' homes to build family rapport, encourage attendance, and address issues affecting education
  • Enrolment Support: Help new students — especially from underserved communities or first-generation learner families — enrol and settle into school
  • Bridge Course: Run short-term catch-up programs for students who are academically behind or rejoining after a break
  • Library & Reading: Manage a school book corner and run regular reading sessions to build the habit of reading
  • NMMS Preparation: Identify eligible students and support their preparation for the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship exam
  • School Annual Day: Assist in planning and executing school functions, involving students, teachers, and families
  • NGO Collaboration: Coordinate with other NGOs or organisations to bring additional resources, volunteers, or programs to the school
  • Weekly Report: Submit an activity report every Friday and attend the Vazhai weekly status call Mandatory
  • Yearly Report: Prepare a comprehensive annual report covering student progress, school activities, and key challenges Mandatory
  • School Cleaning & Tree Plantation: Participate in cleanliness drives and tree-planting activities with student involvement — building ownership of the school environment
🗺️
Field Coordinators
Employee · On-ground management across multiple schools

Field Coordinators are Vazhai's on-ground managers. They visit schools weekly, ensure School Companions are on track, identify new schools, and bridge the gap between schools and the broader Vazhai volunteer network.

  • Visit at least one school per week — speak with the Headmaster, identify gaps, and review the School Companion's weekly report against goals Mandatory
  • Identify and visit new schools in surrounding areas; fill school identification forms and gather contact details, requirements, images and videos Mandatory
  • Train teachers and School Companions for student level identification (3× a year); coordinate with Aid India and other experts Mandatory
  • Conduct career guidance sessions every year in every school we work with — hire required experts Mandatory
  • Plan and conduct inter-school competitions including sports and activities like speech and writing Mandatory
  • Enable skill training visits for students (e.g., visits to TVS and similar organisations) Mandatory
  • Visit potential donors and assist with fundraising; keep the Vazhai Volunteer Group updated on all field activities Mandatory

Our Earlier Programs · Still Active

Direct Mentoring & Life Skills

From 2005 to 2024, Vazhai ran continuous mentoring programs in Dharmapuri and Villupuram — building real relationships with students through workshops, home visits, and career guidance sessions. Over 500 students benefited, many going on to higher education and careers they hadn't imagined before. These programs continue to complement our new school support model.

Geographic Focus

Our earlier work covered Dharmapuri and Villupuram districts. Our new school support initiative begins in the remote hill and forest areas beyond Aiyur near Denkanikottai (Krishnagiri district) — villages lying 20–30 km inside forest routes with limited or no transport. We started with Kodakarai Secondary School in 2025 and plan to expand to 10 schools in 2026, with more regions to follow.

Each school added means a School Companion present every day — not a one-off visit, but a sustained, year-round relationship with students, teachers, and families.

Ways to contribute

What Volunteers Work On

Level Identification Materials: Review and standardise student assessment sheets using the AID India framework — a focused 2–4 hour task, done remotely, needed before June 2026.

School Identification Form: Design a structured field visit form — school profile, infrastructure, staff count, student strength, needs, contact details. A one-time contribution used by every coordinator going forward.

Stage Speech Content: Create topic suggestions, sample scripts, and short demo videos for student stage speeches — directly used by School Companions week to week.

Career Exposure Sessions: Share your professional journey in a 30-minute session — via video call or in person. Engineers, doctors, teachers, tradespeople — every profession opens a new horizon for children who have rarely met anyone from outside their village.

Bridge Course Materials: Develop simple Tamil-medium catch-up content for students behind grade level — especially in reading comprehension and basic numeracy.

ITI & NCSC Preparation: Help 10th standard students apply for the Ashok Leyland ITI Integrated Course, or mentor students through National Children's Science Congress project work.

Digital Skills for Companions: Help School Companions get comfortable with reporting tools, Google Forms for data collection, or basic spreadsheets — small training sessions with large operational benefit.

Fundraising & Donor Outreach: Use your professional network to identify donors, corporate CSR opportunities, or grant programs — and help us make the case for Vazhai.

Photography & Storytelling: Visiting a school? Document it — photos, short videos, student moments. Every authentic piece of content helps communicate our work to people who haven't been there.

First Generation Graduates are especially welcome. Your journey — from where you started to where you are — is the most powerful story you can share with a child standing at the same crossroads.
