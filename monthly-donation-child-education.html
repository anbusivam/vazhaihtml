Monthly Child Education Sponsorship

Sponsor Child Education Every Month

Your monthly gift helps Vazhai place trained School Companions (teachers appointed by Vazhai) in rural government schools in Tamil Nadu. Choose an amount below to help children receive consistent learning, guidance, and care throughout the school year.

What a School Companion does

In schools that do not have enough teachers, a School Companion provides consistent academic and personal support by:

  • Conducting regular classes and identifying each student's learning level.
  • Tracking progress and helping bridge foundational learning gaps.
  • Mentoring students through challenges linked to geographical isolation and life in rural hilly areas.
  • Speaking with parents and helping prevent dropouts caused by learning gaps, family difficulties, or financial pressure.
