The Problem We're Solving

What rural schools face daily

01

Geography as isolation

Schools sit 20–30 km inside forest roads. Teachers don't stay. Many primary schools have just one teacher for every grade.

02

Silent dropout

Parents migrate for daily wages, sometimes taking children. Dropout doesn't announce itself — a child just stops showing up.

03

No roadmap

First-generation learners with no one at home who's been through school or college. They don't know what's possible.

04

Compounding gaps

A child who can't read by Class 5 rarely catches up alone. Without targeted support, the gap widens every year.

Our Story · Values · People

Who We Are

VAZHAI was founded in April 2005 in Chennai by first-generation graduates from Presidency College who knew what it cost to be the first in a family to reach higher education — and chose to lower that cost for everyone who came after.

"We were first-generation graduates ourselves. We knew the confusion, the lack of guidance, the moments where you almost gave up. We started Vazhai so other children wouldn't have to go through that alone."
Vazhai Founding Team · Presidency College, Chennai · Est. April 2005
🌿
Vazhai means banana tree in Tamil — it gives completely and regenerates on its own.

Every part of the banana tree serves a purpose. One child who gets an education passes that possibility to the next generation — in Krishnagiri, Dharmapuri, Villupuram, and beyond. We build literacy, confidence, career awareness, and community trust — because a child needs all of it to thrive.

Our Three Principles
I
Generational Growth
One child's education passes to the next. We build that cycle, school by school.
II
Complete Nourishment
Literacy, confidence, career awareness, and community trust — a child needs all of it.
III
Built to Last
A permanent commitment to rural education equity — one framework, many schools, sustained presence.

"Give a man a fish and you feed him for a day. Teach him how to fish and you feed him for a lifetime."

Education is not a gift you give once — it is a skill that compounds across a lifetime. That is the foundation Vazhai is built on: not charity, but capability. Not one meal, but the knowledge to never go hungry again.

Where We Work

Krishnagiri · Dharmapuri · Villupuram

Between 2005 and 2024, Vazhai served students in Dharmapuri and Villupuram. In 2025, we expanded into the forest and hill region beyond Aiyur, near Denkanikottai in Krishnagiri district — where some villages sit 20–30 km inside forest routes.

We began with Kodakarai Secondary School. By the end of 2026, we aim to have 10 rural government schools covered — not a workshop that comes and goes, but a person who stays.

What Volunteers Actually Do
📋
Student Assessment Materials
Review and standardise student level identification sheets using the AID India framework. 2–4 hour remote task. Needed before June 2026.
🎤
Career Exposure Sessions
Share what you do in a 30-minute call. Engineer, nurse, chef, accountant — any profession is valuable to a child who has never met someone from outside their village.
📖
Bridge Course Content
Create simple Tamil-medium catch-up lessons for students one or two grades behind. Plain language, practical examples.
🔬
ITI & NCSC Support
Help 10th standard students apply for the Ashok Leyland ITI Integrated Course or guide science congress project work.
📣
Fundraising & CSR Outreach
Connect Vazhai to donors, corporate volunteering programs, payroll giving schemes, or CSR education projects. Your network is our reach.
