A day to remember

Celebrating together in Bettamugilallam and Siddapuram

Vazhai conducted Independence Day events at Bettamugilallam and Siddapuram schools, bringing children, teachers, School Companions, and families together.

The celebrations gave students space to participate, perform, and feel proud of their schools. Small moments of shared joy are part of building a school community that children want to return to.
