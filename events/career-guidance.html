Career Guidance & College Pathways

Not just one conversation: help rural students explore their futures, choose suitable colleges and complete applications after Class 12.

The path ahead

Guidance in December. Support beyond the day.

December 2026 · Career guidanceHelp students understand courses, careers and pathways after school. The session will take place on a weekday, so working volunteers may need to take leave. The exact date, time and venue are to be confirmed.
College research · Start nowBuild a practical shortlist for students completing Class 12, based on their interests, marks, family circumstances and goals. You can help with research even if you cannot attend the weekday session.
Applications · As admissions openAfter the guidance session, help students check eligibility, admission deadlines, required documents, scholarships and counselling steps, then follow through on applications.
Colleges that help students move forward

Look beyond the college brochure

Prioritise affordable, supportive colleges with credible routes to employment. Verify information from official sources and actual outcomes rather than relying only on advertisements. No college can guarantee a job.

Learning and employmentCheck course recognition, teaching quality, skills training, internships and verified placement outcomes: what jobs graduates get, not just a placement percentage.
The full costCompare fees, scholarships, hostel charges and travel costs so students and families understand what is affordable.
Support for rural and first-generation studentsLook at safety, mentoring, language support for Tamil-medium learners and practical help adjusting to college life.
A choice that fits the studentMatch options to each student's interests, eligibility and circumstances. Consider college, ITI and other training pathways without treating one route as right for everyone.

Help with one stage, or stay for the journey.

Register and select career conversations, education and training guidance, or both. Mention your weekday availability and whether you can help with college research or applications. Already registered? Contact the team to offer your help.
