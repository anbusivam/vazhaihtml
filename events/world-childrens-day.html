20 November 2026 · A day to remember

World Children's Day with Vazhai

Vazhai volunteers joined students, teachers and school teams across seven Krishnagiri government schools for drawing, crafts, speeches, games and life skills.

Denkanikottai area · Krishnagiri · Tamil Nadu
7government schools
1 dayof learning together

The storyPhoto notesTeachers' voicesThe seven schools
More than a celebration

A chance to see what children can do

One head teacher told us they had not realised how much their students could do. Seeing the children take part had made them very happy. Another had already shared the day in a group and felt people would be talking about it for the next week.

That was the heart of Vazhai's World Children's Day celebration on 20 November 2026. In seven government schools near Denkanikottai in Krishnagiri district, children spent the day speaking, drawing, making crafts, playing games and practising everyday life skills. The joy was in taking part. The teachers' feedback reminded us how much a day like this can reveal.

Before the school bell

Different journeys. One shared morning.

The Bengaluru team set off at 4 in the morning. Volunteers from Chennai travelled overnight. Everyone met for breakfast in Denkanikottai, where the organising team went through the day's plan and the care to take in each school.

Then the teams took their school kits and refreshments and headed out. By 9 in the morning, they had reached their schools. A month of preparation was about to become a day with the children.

Little moments from the day

Make something. Try something. Share it.

Children enjoyed the drawing, craft and life skills sessions. Paper became colourful creations. Drawing sheets filled with trees, homes and imagined landscapes. There was room to work with their hands, share ideas and learn together.

Space for every voice

A little guidance before stepping forward

At Kodagarai Government High School, guest trainer Ramya Ashok of Ramyam Learning Academy worked with students before their speeches. A public speaker and speech trainer, she gave this school focused support as children prepared to speak.

Deepthi, a new Vazhai volunteer with skills in crafts and drawing, joined as another special guest. Her contribution added to the activity guidance that volunteers had discussed in the preparation meetings.

What stayed with the teachers

Their happiness mattered most

In a WhatsApp message, the acting head teacher of Government High School, Kodagarai, thanked the team for spending the day alongside the students. They described how the sessions brought out children's speaking, craft and drawing skills and gave them happiness and confidence.

The message said the time spent with the children and the things given to them were priceless to the school. It ended with thanks to the Vazhai family and a wish that it would keep growing.

The care behind the day

Good preparation made room for the children

Work began a month earlier. Weekly planning calls and smaller discussions gave volunteers time to propose activities, review ideas and agree on the sessions. Our field coordinator also followed up with education officers and secured permission before the event.

The day before, Vinoth, Ramesh, Loganathan and the organising team packed the school kits. Materials were sorted by class and instructor. Each team had a clear activity chart with the session order and guidance for speeches, drawing, life skills, icebreakers and games.

Exam pads, notebooks, pens, rulers, erasers and handkerchiefs were ready for the children. Classes 1 and 2 also had black slates. These were useful learning materials for everyday school life. The life skills session included a reminder about using a handkerchief.

Special thanks to lead volunteer Vinoth for bringing so much care to the plan. With the details ready, volunteers could give their attention to the children.

One celebration across seven schools

Where we spent the day

The celebration took place in these government schools in the Denkanikottai area of Krishnagiri district, Tamil Nadu.

  • GHS, Kodagarai
  • PUPS, Bettamugalalam
  • PUPS, Kodagarai
  • PUPS, Kotchavur
  • PUMS, Siddapuram
  • PUMS, Bolakollai
  • PUMS, Kottaiyur

GHS means Government High School. PUPS means Panchayat Union Primary School. PUMS means Panchayat Union Middle School.

The day did not end with the activities

Recognition, reflection and the road ahead

Around 3 in the afternoon, winners across classes received prizes. Each school also recognised an overall winner and runner up. Every child received a Vazhai certificate signed by the school authority.

Later, the teams gathered at Siddapuram school over tea and snacks. We shared what went well and what needed to improve. Feedback from head teachers, acting head teachers, teachers and volunteers was very positive. We also began talking about December's career guidance and college admission support.

From Siddapuram, volunteers travelled to ARCOD's Namma Veedu in Royakottai. School Companions, school coordinators and training team members stayed for the separate School Companion training on 21 and 22 November. Others returned to Bengaluru. Some Chennai volunteers left on Saturday after staying for a night.

A day like this does not tell us everything about a child's future. But it gives children a chance to try, and adults a chance to notice. We came home grateful for both.
