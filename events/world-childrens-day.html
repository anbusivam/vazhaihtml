The programme plan

A school celebration built around children's voices

The programme was planned for 861 students across seven government schools in the Krishnagiri area, with storytelling, speeches, drawing, everyday life skills, games, prizes and refreshments. Activities were designed to suit different ages and each school's space and timetable.

The figures above describe the original plan, not confirmed attendance.
