World Children's Day at school

A day of school celebrations across seven communities, shaped around children's voices, creativity and joy.

✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦
861students planned
7government schools
Why this day matters

A school celebration where every child can take part

On 20 November 2026, Vazhai is planning World Children's Day workshops across seven government schools in the Krishnagiri area. Each school will host a one-day programme, adapted to its students, space and timetable.

The aim is more than a fun day. Children get room to speak in their own words, try something creative, learn useful everyday habits and enjoy being part of a team. Activities are planned for different age groups so younger children and older students can participate in ways that suit them.

A day of learning and play

What children will get to do

Speak with confidenceStorytelling for younger students and speech topics for older students, with practice supported by teachers.
Make and drawAge-appropriate drawing and colouring activities invite children to add their own ideas and details.
Learn everyday habitsInteractive sessions introduce toothbrushing, handwashing, handkerchief use and, for older groups, personal safety and respectful communication.
Play togetherShort group games include an ice-breaker, animal-action play for younger children and team balloon challenges for older students.
Celebrate effortStudents will be recognised in speech and drawing competitions, with prizes planned by class group.
Share a moment togetherA snack break and a thank-you to teachers and school leaders bring the school day together.

Activities and groupings may vary by school to fit the space, student numbers and age groups.

Help make the day possible.

Donations help cover learning materials, drawing and activity supplies, certificates, prizes and refreshments for the seven schools. Volunteers can also contribute their time and skills.

The people making it happen

Schools, teachers and volunteers

Vazhai's School Companions coordinate with each school and help shape a practical plan with its teachers. Volunteers will lead activities, guide student groups, prepare materials and help the day run smoothly. Teachers support students as they prepare and make sure activities fit their school.

After the celebration, Vazhai's team will review what worked well and what could be improved, so the learning can inform future programmes.

Continuing the learning

School Companion training · 21–22 November

The celebration on 20 November is followed by a separate two-day training programme for newly inducted School Companions, with sessions from BHUMI trainers and Vazhai volunteers.
