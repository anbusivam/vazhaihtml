The day we are planning

A celebration that reaches every child

On 20 November, Vazhai will bring the joy of World Children's Day to seven government schools in the Krishnagiri forest area. For many children, a school celebration is a rare chance to gather, be seen, and enjoy learning together.

The day will be simple, warm, and full of participation. School Companions and volunteers will help each school create a memory that belongs to the children.

What is planned
SpeechesChildren and school leaders open the day together.
Drawing competitionsA chance for every child to imagine and create.
Games and snacksTime to play, share, and celebrate friendship.
Prize distributionEvery effort gets encouragement and applause.

Your donation helps cover the shared celebration across all seven schools. Your time can help us plan, prepare, and show up.
