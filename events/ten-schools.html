Building on our first seven schools

The earlier phase of our work reached Bettamugilallam, Kottaiyur, Bollakollai, Siddapuram, Kodakarai's primary and secondary schools, and Kotchavur. The photographs below are from the earlier School Companion appointments.

School Companions support foundational learning, track attendance, work with headmasters, and visit families when a child starts to disappear from school.
