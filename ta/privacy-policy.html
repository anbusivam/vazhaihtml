Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you make a donation, sign up as a volunteer, or contact us through the website, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Mailing address
  • PAN (Permanent Account Number) — for donation receipts
  • Any comments or messages you include

We also collect basic technical data such as page visits and browser type through Google Analytics and Turnstile for analytics and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Processing and receipting donations via Razorpay
  • Communicating about your donation or subscription
  • Evaluating volunteer or job applications
  • Responding to inquiries via email or the contact page
  • Sending occasional updates (only if you have opted in)
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Payment Data

All payment processing is handled by Razorpay, a PCI DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateway. We never store or have access to your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or bank account details. Your payment data is transmitted directly to Razorpay over TLS 1.2+ encryption.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your data only with:

  • Razorpay — for payment processing (name, email, phone, PAN, amount)
  • Google Analytics — anonymized browsing data for site analytics
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — bot detection and fraud prevention

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of Vazhai, its donors, or beneficiaries.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your personal data is stored securely in Vazhai's internal systems and on Netlify's infrastructure. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, or destruction. PAN data is stored with restricted access and is retained for the period required by Indian tax law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Withdraw consent for future communications

To exercise these rights, email us at vazhai.connect@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for Google Analytics (anonymized) and Cloudflare Turnstile (security). We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or marketing. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect certain site functionality.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., WhatsApp, Razorpay). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us:

Email: vazhai.connect@gmail.com
Contact page: vazhai.in/contact
