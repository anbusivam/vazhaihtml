Welcome

வாழை (Vazhai) represents generational growth. We nurture the path to education and success for children in the remotest parts of Tamil Nadu.

Our Genesis

Founded in 2005 by First Generation graduates from Presidency College Chennai. We believe in paying back to society through mentoring and guidance.

The Symbol

The banana tree (Vazhai) symbolizes total utility and the continuous cycle of life and support.

Our Core Programs

  • Academic Mentoring
  • Life Skills Workshops
  • Career Guidance
