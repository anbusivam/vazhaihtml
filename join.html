Volunteer

Join Vazhai

Vazhai works alongside rural government-school students to make learning feel possible and futures feel open. You can help make that happen without moving to a village or committing every week: bring one useful skill, one conversation, or a few hours a month, and we will help turn it into something a child can use.

Choose your way to help

Make your contribution count

Pick the kind of involvement that fits your life. We will give you a clear brief, a real person to work with, and a task that reaches students.

📋
Make learning materials
Help review and improve student assessment sheets and simple learning resources. A focused 2–4 hour remote task can help us understand what each child needs next.
🎤
Share your world
Join a 30-minute online career conversation. Engineer, nurse, chef, accountant — your story can make a child’s future feel bigger and more possible.
📖
Help a child catch up
Create clear Tamil-medium lessons and practical exercises for students who are one or two grades behind. Good explanations matter more than fancy materials.
🔬
Guide a student project
Help a 10th standard student explore the Ashok Leyland ITI Integrated Course or bring a science congress project to life.
🎓
Help a student apply to college
Help a student find and apply for courses that fit their marks, interests, background, and goals. Make the options clear, check the requirements, and support them through the application.
📝
Help a student register for work
Guide a student through Employment Exchange registration so they can access relevant job opportunities, training, and updates.
📣
Open doors for Vazhai
Introduce us to a donor, company, volunteering program, or CSR team. Your network can put the right support in reach of a rural school.
How It Works

Join in 4 steps

01
Tell us what you can offer
Fill our five-minute form with your skills, interests, and availability. There is no perfect volunteer profile.
02
Meet the Vazhai team
We answer your questions and find the contribution that fits your time and experience.
03
Start with one clear task
You will know what to do, who it helps, and when it is needed — never a vague request to “help out.”
04
See the work move forward
Stay connected through our Friday calls, hear from the field, and see how your contribution becomes part of a child’s journey.
Start with one small contribution
Tell us what you enjoy doing and how much time you have. We will get back to you within a few days with a place to begin.
