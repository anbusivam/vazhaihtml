Volunteer

Give a few hours. Help a student move forward.

Vazhai works alongside rural government-school students. Choose one practical way to help: no move to a village and no weekly commitment required. Bring one useful skill, one conversation, or a few hours a month.

Explore ways to help ↓ We will suggest one clear place to begin.
Registered in Tamil Nadu since 2005 Based in Krishnagiri Flexible remote opportunities
Support students directly
📋
Make learning materials and help a child catch up
Help review assessment sheets, create clear Tamil-medium lessons, and prepare practical exercises for students who are one or two grades behind. A focused 2–4 hour remote task can help us understand what each child needs next.
🎤
Conduct career guidance sessions
Join a 30-minute online career guidance session. Share what it is like to be an engineer, nurse, chef, accountant, or another professional, and help a child see more possible futures.
🎓
Guide students towards ITI, college, and other pathways
Help students explore and apply for courses that fit their marks, interests, background, and goals, including the Ashok Leyland ITI Integrated Course. Make the options clear, check the requirements, and support them through the next step.
📝
Help students register with the Employment Exchange and find job opportunities
Guide students through Employment Exchange registration so they can access relevant job opportunities, training, and updates.
Understand school needs
🧭
Help with school and community field surveys
Listen to students, families, and teachers, document what schools and communities need, and help us shape support around what we learn.
Strengthen Vazhai’s reach
📣
Open doors for Vazhai
Introduce us to a donor, company, volunteering program, or CSR team. Your network can put the right support in reach of a rural school.
🌱
Join, understand, and find your way
Join us first, observe how Vazhai works, and then choose an area listed here, suggest a different way to help, or shape your own good idea with us.
