Thank you, donors

✦ From all of us at Vazhai

Every gift — big or small — keeps a child in school.

Our mission is simple: a trained, full-time School Companion in every remote government school in rural Tamil Nadu — so no child is left without a mentor beside them. Every donation that reached us in the last 12 months moved us closer to that goal. Some gifts were large, some were modest. To the children in our schools, every single one matters.

💛 Thank you for being part of this journey.
🌱 Small gifts, big roots
A small monthly gift buys study materials, stationery, and books for a child for a whole year.
🌳 Big gifts, strong trees
A larger gift keeps a trained School Companion present in a classroom — day after day, all year.
💛 Every gift, one shared story
Both are the same forest. Both are Vazhai. Every donor is part of why a child walks into school smiling.
